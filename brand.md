# EvntX brand

## Positioning
We help **NZ clubs, venues and community groups** solve **the council permits, licences and deadlines that stand between them and their event** through **one plain-English description that becomes a checked, council-ready pack**.

- Who is this for: volunteer organisers at sports clubs, schools, markets, venues and community trusts. They run one or two events a year and are not permit experts.
- What problem does it solve: nobody tells them which documents their event needs, the council rules change, and the deadlines (working days, the 20 Dec to 15 Jan liquor period) are easy to miss.
- Why is it different: rules and dates come from checked council sources, not a chatbot's memory. Every requirement shows its source and the date we last checked it. The pack is checked against the council's own checklist before anyone lodges it.
- Why should anyone care: a missed deadline or a missing document cancels the event.

## Messaging
- Hero headline: Describe your event once. Get a council-ready pack.
- Subheadline: Tell us what you're planning in your own words. EvntX works out what the council needs, drafts it, checks it against their checklist and keeps every deadline in view.
- Brand attributes: plain, sure-footed, on your side.

## Visual identity
- Interface typeface: Inter (all UI, body, labels, buttons).
- Display typeface: Bricolage Grotesque, 700 to 800, optical size 96 at display sizes. Confident, a little quirky, friendly. Page titles, hero, big numbers only.
- Type scale: 1.25 ratio, 16px base: 12, 13, 16, 20, 25, 31, 39, 49, 61, 76.
- Primary colour: Harbour `#2447D9` (buttons, links, focus, active step). Ink `#141724` for text. Neutrals are Harbour-tinted.
- Marker `#D6E0FF`: the highlighter blue behind phrases EvntX understood. This is the brand's signature. Use it only for "this came from your words".
- Semantic colours: pass `#157347`, needs a fix `#C0271D`, warning `#9A5B00`. Never used for brand moments. Always paired with an icon or a word.
- Radius personality: balanced. 10px base. Inputs and buttons 8 to 10px, tags 6px, the site plan canvas 14px. Not one radius for everything.
- Structure: no cards. Ruled rows, list and detail splits, whitespace and type for hierarchy. One primary button per view.
- Logo approach: wordmark in Bricolage Grotesque 800. App icon: a Harbour rounded square with a Marker bar and a white tick (`app/icon.svg`).

Tokens: `design-tokens/tokens.css` is generated by `.claude/skills/saas-brand-system/scripts/generate_tokens.py` (command at the top of `app/globals.css`). Change a colour there, not in a component.

## Motion
Emil Kowalski's rules. Custom curves only: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`. UI under 300ms. Buttons press to `scale(0.97)`. Navigation (stepper, tabs) never animates. One orchestrated moment on the landing page (the marker sweep). The red-to-green checklist fix is the one earned celebration in the app. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns it all off.

## Tone of voice
| Attribute | We say | We don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | "You need a special licence because alcohol will be sold." | "Based on your inputs, a licensing obligation has been identified." |
| Sure-footed | "Lodge by 29 Jan. The legal minimum is 15 Feb." | "You may want to consider lodging at some point before your event." |
| On your side | "You handle this one. It's the council's own form." | "This document is not supported." |

## Microcopy rules
- Buttons are verbs describing the outcome: "Check my event", "Apply fix", "Send me the reminder now", "Create Eventbrite draft".
- An action keeps its name through the flow: "Apply fix" then "Fixed".
- Errors say what happened and how to fix it, in the interface's voice. No apologies.
- Empty states explain the next action.
- Sources read "Source: CCC event permits, checked 26 Sep 2026". Never invent a fee, name, number or date. Unknown is "varies, check with council" or a `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps labels, no "Unlock the power of".
